Symbols

Symbols of Block Diffusion Rooflines: every symbol, its meaning and where it is defined.

The symbols of Block Diffusion RooflinesPrevious: A · Appendix

This page lists every symbol of the blogpost, with its meaning and the place where it is introduced. The values in parentheses are those of DiffusionGemma 26B-A4B and of an H200. In the other chapters, a click on a formula lists the symbols that it uses.

Decoding

Symbol Meaning Introduced
\(\Gamma\) throughput gain: tokens per second of TD over those of AR Proposition 1
\(B\) batch: sequences decoded together Part 1
\(C\) context: tokens already in the KV cache of each sequence Part 1
\(b\) canvas length, in tokens (256) Part 1
\(S\) denoising passes per canvas (14) Part 1
\(r\) cost of a denoising pass, as a fraction of a forward pass (1) Part 1
\(\omega = Sr + 1\) forward passes that one canvas costs (15) Part 1
\(\mathrm{TPF} = b/\omega\) tokens committed per forward pass (17.07) Part 1
\(P,\ G\) prompt tokens and generated tokens per sequence (1,024 and 8,192) Part 6
\(\lambda\) logit-sized reads and writes of HBM per output position (5 for TD, 2 for AR); 0 means chunked logits Part 6

Accelerator and precision

Symbol Meaning Introduced
\(\pi\) peak FLOPs per second of the accelerator, on matrix multiplications (989 TFLOP/s on an H200) Part 1
\(\beta\) memory bandwidth of the accelerator, in bytes per second (4.8 TB/s on an H200) Part 1
\(I^\star = \pi/\beta\) ridge point of the accelerator: a pass is compute-bound when its intensity reaches it (206 on an H200) Definition 3
\(s_w\) bytes per weight (2 in BF16, 16-bit floats; 1 in FP8, 8-bit floats) Part 1
\(s_{kv}\) bytes per element of the KV cache (2) Part 1
\(\kappa^c\) cached tensors per KV head on the global layers (2: keys and values stored separately) Part 1

Model

Symbol Meaning Introduced
\(L\) layers (30) Definition 1
\(d\) hidden size (2816) Part 4
\(V\) vocabulary size (262,144) Part 6
\(\mathcal G,\ \mathcal W,\ \mathcal S\) sets of the global-attention, sliding-window and sparse layers (5, 25 and none in DiffusionGemma) Part 1
\(w\) window of a sliding-window layer, which sees at most \(w\) earlier tokens (1,023) Part 1
\(n_q,\ n_{kv,\ell}\) query heads and KV heads of a layer (16 query heads; 2 KV heads on the global layers, 8 on the window layers) Part 1
\(h_\ell\) head dimension of layer \(\ell\) (512 on the global layers, 256 on the window layers) Part 1
\(g\) query heads per KV head on the global layers, \(n_q/n_{kv,\ell}\) (8) Part 1
\(E\) experts per MoE layer (128) Definition 1
\(k\) experts each token selects (8) Definition 1
\(W^e\) parameters of one expert in one layer, \(3d\,f_E\) (5.95M) Definition 1
\(f_E\) width of one expert (704) Part 10
\(W_{\mathrm{ne}}\) non-expert parameters of all layers (1.66B for DiffusionGemma) Definition 1
\(N_{\mathrm{act}}\) parameters active per token, \(W_{\mathrm{ne}} + LkW^e\) (3.08B for DiffusionGemma, without the vocabulary) Definition 2
\(W_{\mathrm{tot}}\) all parameters, \(W_{\mathrm{ne}} + LEW^e\) (24.5B, without the vocabulary) Part 3
\(m\) cached tokens each query selects in a sparse layer (2048 in the sparse variant) Part 1
\(n_{I,\ell}\) heads of the indexer of sparse layer \(\ell\), \(n_I\) when the layer is clear (8) Definition 1
\(h_{I,\ell},\ s_I\) head dimension of the indexer of sparse layer \(\ell\) (\(h_I\) when the layer is clear) and bytes per element of its keys (128, 1 in FP8) Definition 1

Costs and intensities

Symbol Meaning Introduced
\(F,\ Q\) FLOPs and bytes of one pass, per sequence Definition 2
\(\nu\) KV-cache bytes per cached token (G: global, W: windowed, S: sparse layers, I: indexer) Definition 1
\(\alpha\) attention FLOPs per query and per cached token (same subscripts as \(\nu\)) Definition 1
\(\class{mj-ar}{W_{AR}}\) weights an AR step reads at batch \(B\), in parameters Definition 2
\(\class{mj-td}{W_{TD}}\) weights a TD pass reads at batch \(B\), in parameters Definition 2
\(U_{E,k}(N)\) expected number of distinct experts that \(N\) positions touch in a layer (\(E = 128\) experts, \(k = 8\) per token) Definition 2
\(U_{C,m}(N)\) cached tokens that \(N\) queries read together in a sparse layer, each selecting \(m\): the coverage function \(U_{n,k}\) of Definition 1 with \(n = C\) and \(k = m\), so \(U_{C,m}(1) = \min(C, m)\) Definition 2
\(\class{mj-ar}{I_{AR}}\) arithmetic intensity of one AR step: its FLOPs per byte read from HBM Definition 3
\(\class{mj-td}{I_{TD}}\) arithmetic intensity of one TD pass over a canvas of \(b\) positions: its FLOPs per byte read from HBM Definition 3
\(I^X_{\mathrm{lin}}\) linear part of an intensity: linear-layer FLOPs over weight bytes Definition 3
\(I^X_{\mathrm{attn}}\) attention part of an intensity: attention FLOPs over KV-cache bytes Definition 3
\(I_{\mathrm{lin}}\) linear part of an AR step's intensity, \(\class{mj-ar}{I^{AR}_{\mathrm{lin}}}\) (1.0 at batch 1, 8.2 at batch 64; the batch \(B\) for a dense model in BF16) Definition 3
\(I_{\mathrm{attn}}\) attention FLOPs per cache byte for one query on a global layer (8 for DiffusionGemma) Definition 3
\(I_{\mathrm{win}}\) attention FLOPs per cache byte for one query on a window layer (2 for DiffusionGemma) Definition 3
\(I_{\mathrm{idx}}\) FLOPs per cache byte of a sparse layer's index scan for one query Definition 3
\(\theta\) share of the bytes of a pass carried by one of its parts Proposition 2
\(\theta_E\) expert share of the weights that an AR step reads Proposition 3
\(\sigma\) window layers' FLOPs per query over the linear FLOPs, \(\alpha_W w/(2N_{\mathrm{act}})\) (6.8%) Proposition 6
\(C_F\) context at which global attention alone matches the linear FLOPs (37.6k) Proposition 6

Penalties

Symbol Meaning Introduced
\(\Pi\) overall penalty: bytes per sequence of a TD pass over those of an AR step Definition 4
\(\Pi_{\mathrm{lin}}\) linear penalty: weight bytes a TD pass reads over those an AR step reads (7.9 at batch 1, 1 at large batch) Definition 4
\(\Pi_{\mathrm{attn}}\) attention penalty: cache bytes a TD pass reads over those an AR step reads (1 for dense and windowed attention) Definition 4
\(\Pi_E\) distinct experts a TD pass touches over those an AR step touches Definition 4
\(\Pi_{\mathrm{sel}}\) selection penalty: cached tokens a canvas selects in a sparse layer over those one query selects Definition 4
\(\zeta\) independent fraction of a canvas's selections: 1 when independent, \(1/b\) when every query selects the same tokens Part 4

Thresholds and regimes

Symbol Meaning Introduced
\(\class{mj-td}{B^\star_{TD}}\) smallest batch at which a TD pass is compute-bound Part 3
\(\class{mj-ar}{B^\star_{AR}}\) smallest batch at which an AR step is compute-bound Part 3
\(B_\times\) parity batch: beyond it AR is faster Part 3
\(b_c\) critical canvas length \(I^\star/I_{\mathrm{attn}}\): above it a longer context helps TD reach the ridge Proposition 8
R1–R8 the eight regimes (Figure 16) Part 5
A1–A5 the assumptions of Proposition 1 Part 2
\(C_{\mathrm{lin}}(B, x)\) context below which the weights are at least a share \(x\) of an AR step's bytes Proposition 4
\(B^\star_n(C),\ D_n(C)\) smallest compute-bound batch for \(n\) queries per sequence at context \(C\), and the FLOPs per sequence left for the weights Proposition 8

Measurements

Symbol Meaning Introduced
\(\hat\pi\) empirical compute ceiling: the highest FLOP rate measured on this H200 (for attention, FlashAttention 3's sustained rate) (FP8 1,468, BF16 781, attention 638 TFLOP/s) Definition 5
\(\hat\beta\) empirical bandwidth: 4.32 TB/s measured on this H200 Definition 5
\(\tau_X,\ \tau_c\) empirical roofline time: \(\max(F/\hat\pi,\ Q/\hat\beta)\), of a component \(c\) or of a whole step \(X\) Definition 5
\(t_X,\ t_c\) measured time of a step (\(\class{mj-ar}{t_{AR}}\), \(\class{mj-td}{t_{TD}}\)) or of a component (\(t_c\)) Definition 7
\(\eta_X,\ \eta_c\) efficiency: roofline time over measured time (1 means at the roofline) Definition 7
\(n_X\) tokens a step commits: \(B\) for AR, \(Bb/\omega\) for TD Proposition 9
\(\Gamma_{\mathrm{meas}}\) measured gain: TD tokens per second over AR tokens per second Proposition 9
\(\Gamma_{\mathrm{roof}}\) roofline bound at the measured ceilings and routing Proposition 9
\(U^{\mathrm{meas}}\) distinct experts per layer that a pass touches, measured Definition 6
\(U^{\mathrm{freq}}\) distinct experts predicted from the measured expert frequencies, tokens independent Definition 6
\(N^{\mathrm{eff}}\) effective independent tokens: the \(N\) at which \(U_{E,k}(N)\) equals the measured count Definition 6
\(\rho_e\) frequency with which a token selects expert \(e\) Definition 6
\(\mu\) rows per touched expert, \(Nk/U\) Part 10
\(M_q,\ M_k\) query rows and keys of one attention tile Part 10
\(\mathbf{Z}\) attention scores \(\mathbf{Q}\mathbf{K}^\top\) of one tile Part 10
\(h\) head dimension of a global layer (512) Part 10
\(\eta^\star_c\) target efficiency of a component: that of the best kernel timed alone at one representative shape, 0.8 where none was timed Definition 7
\(p_{i,e}\) routing weight of expert \(e\) for token \(i\) Part 10
