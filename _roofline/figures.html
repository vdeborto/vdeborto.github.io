How the numbers are counted

Every gain in Parts 1–5 is a ratio of roofline bounds, counted in one of three ways.

A roofline time is a lower bound on the time of a pass, so each throughput is an upper bound, and \(\Gamma\) is the ratio of two such bounds, not a measurement. Part 9 compares these bounds with throughput measured in vLLM. The three accountings below differ in what they include, and each number in the blogpost says which one it uses. For DiffusionGemma on an H200:

In the interactive figures and the cards, the intensities \(I^{\mathrm{AR}}\) and \(I^{\mathrm{TD}}\) are those of Definition 3, without the vocabulary, except in the intensity tabs of Figure 15 and the calculator's maps, which use the exact accounting at the end of this chapter; next to an exact gain, the regime of a pass is given instead as the share of the peak compute that it uses. The cards quote the closed-form values of the propositions, at zero context where Part 2 does, unless a readout says exact. A fourth variant, the kernel-by-kernel timing rule of Part 1, can be switched on in the calculator.
